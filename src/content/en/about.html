about us

students who make each other stronger

L.S.K.V. 'Fortius' was founded by and for Leiden students with a passion for strength sports.

our story

from group of friends to association

L.S.K.V. ‘Fortius’ started as a small group of Leiden students who trained together at the USC Leiden. The association has since grown into a close-knit club with members at every level, from complete beginners to people who compete in powerlifting competitions. What has stayed the same: everyone is welcome, regardless of experience.

We really enjoy seeing people do their best and we encourage it too. You don't need to be experienced to be a member, but being passionate helps!

Besides taking part in competitions, we also organise plenty of other activities to get to know each other better. Every month we get together for drinks and other (sporty) activities. Sometimes we go bowling or out on the water on a party boat. We also organise weekends away together, and there is merch for sale. And every now and then we organise competitions ourselves!

what we stand for

Our core values

Accessible

No entry level, no expensive membership barrier. Everyone trains at their own pace.

Stronger together

We train independently, but we motivate and spot each other. The group makes the difference.

Performance & fun

We take training seriously, but always with a good time first.

Student life

Besides training, we also organise get-togethers, outings and activities. L.S.K.V. ‘Fortius’ is more than a sports club.

board & committees

We have the honour of serving as the second board of L.S.K.V. 'Fortius' this year. The board takes part in meetings with all committees, holds its own meetings, keeps thinking about how we can improve the training facilities, handles contact with external parties and much more!

The association currently has 8 committees: the Activities Committee, Merchandise Committee, Association Weekend Committee, Photo Committee, Competition Committee, Training Committee, Website Committee and the Audit Committee (KasCo). Together with all committees we keep the association strong.
