Nice that you're looking at the webpage of the board of the Leiden Student Strength Sports Association ‘Fortius’. The board consists of 5 members, including:

  1. Chair
  2. Secretary
  3. Treasurer
  4. Internal commissioner
  5. External commissioner

what we do

Tasks and responsibilities

Every board member has their own tasks and responsibilities, such as safeguarding the financial health, maintaining contact with external parties, and may also take part in one or more committees. This way the board contributes both to the functioning of the association and to the organisation of activities, competitions and other initiatives. In addition, there are tasks that are taken on jointly, such as supervising the competition plates.

An environment for everyone

We are also working on the further development of Fortius, so that the association can keep growing and support its members better and better. Besides these responsibilities, we also find it important to create a pleasant, safe and accessible environment in which everyone feels welcome. Whether you train recreationally, want to work on your technique or have ambitions to take part in competitions: we want every member to get the space to develop in their own way.

More than sport

After all, an association is not only about sporting performance, but also about the people who form it. We all put in the effort to create a strong bond and atmosphere where everyone feels at home and can develop. We also find it important to spend time together outside of sport, so that it makes the bonds even stronger.

questions or ideas?

we are always open to suggestions

We are always open to suggestions, questions and initiatives from the association. Together we keep building an association where strength athletes of all levels can feel at home.
