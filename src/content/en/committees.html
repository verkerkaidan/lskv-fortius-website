activities committee

What does the ActiCo do?

The activities committee, or ActiCo, is the committee that organises various activities within the association. We organise one to two activities every month.

Think of two kinds of activities: sports and social activities. So we don't just go swimming, play volleyball or do archery, we also organise fun social activities such as a boat party, a beer tasting or a Christmas dinner!

From coming up with and planning activities to the organisation on the day itself: the ActiCo makes sure everything is well arranged. That way we try to put on a fun and varied activity every time.

photo committee

what does the fotoco do?

The FotoCo captures the best moments of our association! Whether it's social activities, exciting competitions or other highlights: the FotoCo is there to capture these moments on camera. The committee and board photos you can find on this page were also taken by them!

The photos pop up in various places, such as on our Instagram and website. Sometimes they are also shared with the participating members after an activity. That way we can not only enjoy all the great moments ourselves, but also look back on them with a smile later!

So do you see a camera pointed at you soon? Don't just smile at the camera, but also at the photographer behind it ;)

audit committee

what does the kastco do?

The KasCo is the committee responsible for auditing the finances of Fortius! Twice a year, before the General Members' Meetings (ALVs), we dive into the association's financial records to check the treasurer's work.

We check whether all income and expenses have been recorded correctly, whether the amounts match the bank statements and whether all expenses can be properly accounted for. Among other things, we check the membership fees, activities and other financial matters within the association.

After our audit we present our findings to the ALV, so that all members gain insight into the financial state of affairs.

In short: the KasCo keeps an independent eye on things and checks whether the treasurer manages Fortius' finances correctly and responsibly!

merchandise committee

what does the merchco do?

Once a year we release a regular merch drop with our familiar Fortius items, such as cotton and polyester shirts, hoodies, tank tops, bags and towels.

In addition, we try to come out with a special drop once or twice a year. For this we bring a new design or introduce new, unique items. That way the Fortius merch keeps renewing itself and there is always something to look forward to!

For several items it is possible to add your own name, so that your merch is truly yours. The personalisation of the items is also already included in the price.

In short: the MerchCo makes sure you walk around in full Fortius style, during and outside your training sessions!

training committee

what does the trainingco do?

The Training Committee works to further develop and strengthen the strength sports community within our association. Our goal is to make knowledge, guidance and opportunities accessible to everyone, whether you are just starting out or already have experience with powerlifting and strength training.

We organise mock meets and powerlifting clinics, where members get the chance to develop their skills, gain competition experience and deepen their knowledge of the sport. We also organise WBO courses to train and prepare members for judging and other important roles at powerlifting competitions.

The committee consists of several members and is always open to new people who are interested in contributing to the development of the association and the strength sports community.

The Training Committee strives to create an environment where professionalism goes hand in hand with a strong sense of community. We want to support members who are motivated to learn, challenge themselves and keep improving. Regardless of experience or starting level, everyone should get the chance to develop and make a contribution.

Ultimately, the goal is to build a knowledgeable, supportive and driven community in which members help each other become better athletes and training partners and keep developing within strength sports.

association weekend committee

what does the weekend committee (vwco) do?

The VWCo organises two association weekends a year. The first weekend takes place at the start of the year (September/October), ideal for new EL CID members. The second follows about half a year later (around March/April), so that newer members, for example spring members, can join in too!

Our weekends are always great fun: every time we go to a different location, stay together in one house and do lots of fun activities, both indoors and outdoors. Think of a pub quiz and other games, a trip to the nearest city, eating together and often a pub crawl. There is usually also a sporty activity: we have been to a climbing forest, done an escape room and recently held our own farmers' Olympics (boerenlympics) in Friesland!

In short: we organise two of the best weekends of the Fortius year, so we hope to see you at our next weekend!
