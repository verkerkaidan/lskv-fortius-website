About us

More than just lifting weights

L.S.K.V. ‘Fortius’ has existed since 2025 and is the Leiden Student Strength Sports Association (Leidse Studenten Krachtsport Vereniging). By students, for students. We train on our own or together, go to competitions, and also organise plenty of fun activities.

"I go to the gym, I don't powerlift"
