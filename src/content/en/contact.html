doubts?

frequently asked questions

I've never powerlifted before, can I still join?

Yes, most members start without any experience. Everyone trains in their own way, and we help each other along with tips. Members also do other strength sports besides powerlifting, everyone is welcome.

I already go to the gym, what's the difference?

The biggest difference is the atmosphere in the gym once you're a member. It feels great when you know almost everyone, and it gives you a little push on days when you feel less like training. There are also activities you can do with your gym friends, and we hold clinics to improve your technique.

How much time does the association take per week?

As much as you want! It's entirely up to you how often and when you want to train (with us) and whether you want to take part in the activities, committees and/or competitions.

When are the association's training hours?
  • Monday: 17:30-19:30
  • Thursday: 17:30-19:30
  • Saturday: 14:00-15:00
Is there a trainer or coach present?

We are running a pilot to provide an association coach. What exactly that will involve is still to be announced. In addition, there is a lot of knowledge within the association that we like to share with each other during training hours.

Do I have to take part in competitions?

No, certainly not. We would absolutely love for you to take part in competitions, and we're happy to help you with it, but it is by no means a requirement.

Do I have to be a student to become a member?

No, you only need to have a USC membership.

I don't speak Dutch, can I still join?

Yes, of course. We are actively searching for ways to include non-Dutch speaking members.
